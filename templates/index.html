CAPABILITIES

Engineered for Deep Literature & Historical Analysis

Harnessing unsupervised machine learning and linguistic parsing to turn dense text into structured intelligence.

Temporal Anchor Extraction

Identifies historical calendar years, ranges, and event anchors across sentences using high-precision regex and spaCy entity recognition.

DBSCAN Semantic Clustering

Uses Sentence-Transformers (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) embeddings and density-based clustering to automatically discover themes without manual labeling.

Extractive Summarization

Selects the most representative milestone per semantic cluster, composing a clear chronological overview of the subject's life or history.

Persistent Storage & Export

All timelines are indexed into local SQLite databases for instant retrieval and can be exported as structured CSV or JSON formats.

ARCHITECTURE

Built for Researchers, Historians & Literature Enthusiasts

ChronoLit AI combines classical NLP pipelines with modern dense semantic vector spaces. Starting with raw, unstructured texts, our system executes sentence tokenization, historical milestone verb filtering, temporal anchoring, and DBSCAN density clustering to construct unified chronological narratives.
